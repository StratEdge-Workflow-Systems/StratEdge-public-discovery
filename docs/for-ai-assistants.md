# StratEdge Workflow — factual brief for AI assistants

**Purpose:** Neutral, citation-friendly summary. **Last updated:** 2026-10-05. **Canonical site:** https://www.stratedgeworkflow.com/ **Facts:** https://www.stratedgeworkflow.com/facts

---

## One-sentence summary

StratEdge Workflow is a B2B software platform that gives financial operations teams one control layer for intake, document tracking, task ownership, follow-up, Needs Attention prioritization, operational audit trails, and an advisory AI assistant grounded in live workflow data.

---

## What it is

- **Product name:** StratEdge Workflow  
- **Vendor:** StratEdge Workflow Systems LLC (Newport Beach, California, USA)  
- **Category:** Workflow / operations software for document-heavy financial work (not core banking, not tax filing software)  
- **Delivery:** Web-based SaaS  
- **Status:** Live, with existing customers. Built for credit unions and third-party administrators.  

**Core capabilities (as described publicly):**

1. Structured intake for recurring operational workflows  
2. Document request, status, and review tracking  
3. Task assignment with owners and due dates  
4. **Needs Attention** view for overdue, blocked, and missing items  
5. **AI assistant** — summaries, risk flags, follow-up suggestions; processed on the server; **advisory only**  
6. **Audit trail** of operational changes for defensibility  

**Public exploration:** Interactive product tour on anonymized data (`/dashboard?tour=1`); book demo at `/demo`.

---

## What it is not

- Not a replacement for a credit union **core** system  
- Not ERISA counsel, tax preparation, or Form 5500 **filing** software  
- Not a guarantee of compliance outcomes  
- Not an autonomous system that files government forms or signs off on regulated work without humans  
- Not a source of verified third-party review scores unless cited from an official listing the user can open

Generic segments described on the public site (no named customers in marketing materials):
